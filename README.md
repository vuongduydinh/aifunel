# Ai Funel

> All-in-one AI-powered sales funnel and marketing automation platform.

**Website:** [aifunel.com](https://aifunel.com)

Ai Funel combines a funnel builder and AI marketing automation in a single system, so businesses and online entrepreneurs can stop juggling many separate tools and run their entire operation from one place.

## Who it's for

- Online entrepreneurs and course creators
- Small businesses and agencies that sell online
- Teams that want to replace a stack of disconnected marketing tools

## Key features

- **CRM** to manage leads and customers in one place
- **Funnel and website builders** for landing pages, checkout and sales flows
- **Multi-channel posting and customer messaging**
- **Automated sales and booking**
- **Affiliate management**
- **Course creation** and a **private community platform** with gamified points
